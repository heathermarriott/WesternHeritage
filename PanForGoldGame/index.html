How to Play

  1. One Scoop: Each play gives you a single pan full of riverbed dirt, gravel, and hidden treasure.
  2. Swirl: Drag your finger around inside the pan to wash the dirt away, just like a real prospector.
  3. Collect: Once dirt clears off a gold flake, nugget, or gem, tap it to add it to your haul. Plain gray rocks are worthless!
  4. Play Again: When the pan is empty, hit "New Scoop" to start a brand new game. Your best score is saved on this device.

How to Play

  1. Climb On: Tap "New Ride" to start a fresh ride out of the chute.
  2. Watch the Tilt: The bull bucks and leans left or right at random. Watch which way your rider is tipping.
  3. Counter-Lean: Hold the lean button on the opposite side of the tilt to shift your weight back toward center.
  4. Watch for the Warning: When the rider starts sliding and the screen flashes red, you're about to lose your grip — counter-lean hard!
  5. Go the Distance: Stay on for a full 8 seconds to score a Qualified Ride — then keep going for bonus time if you dare!
  6. Don't Eat Dirt: Tip too far one way and you're bucked off. Tap "New Ride" to climb back on.
